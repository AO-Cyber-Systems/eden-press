  without a mechanical trigger silently becomes "never" (Pitfall 4). It MUST be a scheduled
  GitHub Action comparing pinned vs latest tags and filing a deduplicated issue.

  explicitly "before any vendored engine/theme asset exists" — themes land in Objective 3
